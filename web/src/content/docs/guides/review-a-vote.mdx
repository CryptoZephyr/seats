## What you need

The wallet that controls your seat and a little testnet NEAR for the network fee. There is no deposit. [Get testnet NEAR from the faucet](https://near-faucet.io/).

## Steps

1. Open `#/seat` and choose `Review vote` beside the proposal.
2. Read the proposal, your delegate's choice, and its one-line reason.
3. Check the clock time and countdown. The change window lasts 2 minutes.
4. If you disagree, choose `Change vote to Approve` or `Change vote to Reject`.
5. To skip the vote, choose `Cancel this vote`, then `Confirm cancellation`. Choose `Keep this vote` to go back.

## What you'll see

The page confirms a successful change and shows a transaction link when available. After the deadline, it shows the vote recorded for your seat.

## If it goes wrong

You can't change or cancel after the window closes. Refresh the page and check the vote record. If the wallet times out, check the transaction history before trying again.
