## What you see

The vote screen shows the proposal, the delegate's choice, its one-line reason, and a window bar draining to the deadline. The deadline appears as a clock time and a countdown.

## What you do

If you agree, do nothing. The vote is cast at the deadline. If you disagree, choose the other option inside the window.

## What Seats does

The contract accepts the override until the deadline. After that it refuses changes, and the vote is cast under your seat's voter account.

## How you know it worked

After the window closes, the screen shows the vote as cast, with the result from the DAO and the receipt.

## If nothing changes

If the window has closed, the vote can't be changed. Read the receipt for what was cast. See [what happens at the deadline](/docs/concepts/what-happens-at-the-deadline).
