## What you need

The seller must name your `.testnet` account and the club must approve the sale. Keep enough testnet NEAR for the exact seat price and network fees. [Get testnet NEAR from the faucet](https://near-faucet.io/).

## Steps

1. Connect the named buyer account.
2. Open `#/buy` and find the seat under `For sale`.
3. Check the price and the displayed 95% seller, 5% club split.
4. Choose `Buy for [amount] NEAR` and approve the wallet request.

## What you'll see

The purchase transaction appears on the page. After it succeeds, the seat is yours. You can open its history and write a new delegate brief.

## If it goes wrong

Only the named buyer can purchase. If the approval expires or the transaction is refused, ask the seller to submit a new offer. After a wallet timeout, refresh and check the seat owner before retrying.
