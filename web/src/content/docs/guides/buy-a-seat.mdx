## What you see

A listing shows the seat number, the price, the seat's history, and what the seat gives. The split between the seller and the club appears before you buy.

## What you do

Choose buy with club approval and confirm the price in your wallet. Your payment is escrowed.

## What the club does

The club votes on the offer. While it waits, the screen shows the tally and the expiry.

## How you know it worked

On approval the seat moves to you, the seller is paid, the club's levy goes to the treasury, and your vote weight starts at 1. The receipt lists each step.

## If nothing changes

If the club declines or the offer expires, your payment is refunded in full. The receipt shows the refund.
