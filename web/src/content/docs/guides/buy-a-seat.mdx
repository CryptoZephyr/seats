## What you see

A listing shows the seat number, named buyer, price, expiry, and 95% seller / 5% club split. Only the named buyer can sign the purchase.

## What you do

Deposit: the exact approved price. The network fee estimate appears before you sign.

## What the club does

The club votes on the offer. While it waits, the screen shows the tally and the expiry.

## How you know it worked

After success the page refreshes your seats. The testnet explorer link shows the transaction.

## If nothing changes

If the approval expires before you buy, the contract refuses the purchase. The seller must submit a new offer.
