## What you see

The club page shows the seat plan, how many seats are held, and the recent decisions. Open seats are marked on the plan.

## What you do

Sign in with a NEAR testnet account and choose Apply on the club page. The application proposal uses the DAO's current bond, which is returned when the club decides. Follow its tally and deadline on the proposal page.

After approval, claim the seat before the application expires. Claiming attaches 4.5 NEAR to fund your delegate, plus gas. Nearly all of the deposit is returned after each vote. If your balance is short, get testnet NEAR from a NEAR testnet faucet.

## What the club pays for

The claim deposit funds the delegate account and its voting costs. The wallet shows the deposit and gas before you sign.

## How you know it worked

Once the club approves, your seat shows on your seat page, with its number and the date it was held from. The receipt lists each on-chain step with an explorer link.

## If nothing changes

The club's vote may still be open. The page shows the approvals so far. If the club declines, you'll see the decision and the receipt.
