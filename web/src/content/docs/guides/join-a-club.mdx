## What you need

A NEAR testnet wallet and enough testnet NEAR for the 0.1 NEAR application bond and network fee. The bond is returned when the club decides. [Get testnet NEAR from the faucet](https://near-faucet.io/).

## Steps

1. Open the club page and connect your testnet wallet.
2. Under `Your application`, choose `Apply` and approve the wallet request.
3. Watch the application tally and deadline. The club vote can take up to 10 minutes.
4. After approval, return to `Your application` before the approval expires. Choose `Mint your seat` and approve the 4.5 NEAR deposit. This funds your delegate, and almost all of it comes back after each vote.
5. After the seat is minted, choose `Save your delegate's brief`.

## What you'll see

The page shows a transaction link and your new seat number. Your seat page shows its holder and delegate status.

## If it goes wrong

If the application is still open, wait for the club's decision. If approval expires, apply again. If the wallet times out, refresh the page and check whether the transaction landed before you submit again.
