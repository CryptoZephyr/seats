## What you need

The seat number. The history is public and doesn't require a connected wallet.

## Steps

1. On the club page, choose `View history` beside a seat. On `#/seat`, choose `View seat history`. You can also open `#/seat/<id>/history` and replace `<id>` with the seat number.
2. Under `Holders`, check who held the seat and when.
3. Under `Sales`, check completed sale prices and the 95/5 split.
4. Under `Seat <id>'s delegate votes`, check each vote, its status, and its receipt link.
5. Choose `View transaction` to open a public transaction record.

## What you'll see

The page is titled `Seat <id> history`. It lists holder changes, sales, and votes recorded for that seat.

## If it goes wrong

If the history won't load, choose `Retry`. A vote or sale may not appear until its transaction is recorded.
