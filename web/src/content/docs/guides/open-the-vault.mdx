## What you see

The vault screen checks your testnet seat and shows the current maximum execution deposit and expected charge before signing.

## What you do

Choose Open vault. Your browser generates an X25519 key pair in memory and signs a request to the OutLayer testnet contract. The private key is discarded when the page closes.

## What Seats does

The vault job checks that your account holds a seat. If it does, it returns the vault content encrypted to a key only your browser holds. If it doesn't, it refuses.

## How you know it worked

The contract estimate plus a 500,000,000,000 yoctoNEAR buffer is attached. The expected charge is about 0.002 testnet NEAR, with most of the deposit returned. The browser decrypts the job result locally.

## If you are not a holder

You see the refusal: only current seat holders can open the vault. Former members are refused by design. In the testnet run, the former seller's attempt returned `ERR_NO_SEAT`.
