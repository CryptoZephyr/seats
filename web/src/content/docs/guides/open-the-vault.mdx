## What you need

A current seat and the wallet that holds it. Keep testnet NEAR for the vault charge and network fee. [Get testnet NEAR from the faucet](https://near-faucet.io/).

## Steps

1. Open `#/vault` and connect the seat holder wallet.
2. Check the estimated charge and deposit shown on `Club vault`.
3. Choose `Open vault` and approve the wallet request.

## What you'll see

The vault content appears in your browser. Your browser decrypts it with a key held in memory. Leaving the page discards that key. Most of the deposit is returned.

## If it goes wrong

Only the current seat holder can open the vault. If the estimate or network read fails, choose the page's retry action. If the wallet times out, check the transaction before trying again.
