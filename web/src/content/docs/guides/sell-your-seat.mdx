## What you see

Enter an existing buyer's `.testnet` account, a price above zero, and an expiry of 1, 3, or 7 days. The form checks that the buyer account exists and shows the 95% seller / 5% club split before you sign. The DAO proposal bond returns when the club decides.

## What you do

Submit the sale approval proposal. The named buyer can purchase only after the club approves it.

## What ends when the sale completes

Your vote, your vault access, your delegate and its brief, and your delegate's signing key all end. The seat keeps its history, but the buyer starts with a fresh vote.

## How you know it worked

The proposal appears under “Awaiting club vote.” A successful purchase links to its testnet transaction.

## If nothing changes

If the club declines the proposal or the approval expires, the seat stays in your hands.
