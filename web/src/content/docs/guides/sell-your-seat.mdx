## What you need

Hold the seat in the connected wallet. Get the buyer's `.testnet` account and agree on a price. The current proposal bond is 0.1 NEAR and is returned when the club decides. Keep extra testnet NEAR for network fees. [Get testnet NEAR from the faucet](https://near-faucet.io/).

## Steps

1. Open `#/sell` and find `Create a sale offer`.
2. Enter the `Buyer's .testnet account` and `Price in NEAR`.
3. Choose how long the buyer has after approval under `Buyer has ... days after club approval to buy`.
4. Check the displayed split. You receive 95%; the club receives 5%.
5. Choose `Submit sale for club vote` and approve the wallet request.

## What you'll see

The offer appears under `Awaiting club vote`. If the club approves it and the buyer pays in time, the seat moves to the buyer. You receive 95% of the price and the club receives 5%. Your delegate brief and vault access end when the sale completes.

## If it goes wrong

If the club declines or the offer expires, you keep the seat and the buyer is refunded. If the wallet times out, refresh and check the offer before submitting again.
