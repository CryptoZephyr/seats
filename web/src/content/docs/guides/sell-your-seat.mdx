## What you see

The sell screen shows your seat number, a price field, and the split: 95% to you and 5% to the club's treasury.

## What you do

Enter the price and list the seat. The club decides who buys in.

## What ends when the sale completes

Your vote, your vault access, your delegate and its brief, and your delegate's signing key all end. The seat keeps its history, but the buyer starts with a fresh vote.

## How you know it worked

The receipt lists the approval, the escrow, the seat move, your payment, and the levy, each with an explorer link.

## If nothing changes

If the club declines or the offer times out, the buyer is refunded and you keep the seat. Your seat stays in your hands until a sale completes.
