## What you see

Choose a seat you hold, then enter up to 4,000 characters describing what your delegate should back, reject, and any limits. Your current brief isn't shown because it's stored privately. Saving replaces it.

## What you do

1. Confirm in your wallet that you hold the seat. This signature has no fee.
2. The runner encrypts and stores the brief privately in an OutLayer profile.
3. Your wallet saves the fingerprint and profile reference on the seat's voter contract at 30 Tgas with no attached deposit. Network gas still applies.

The service may take up to a minute to wake. The brief text and salt stay out of public testnet reads. Only the fingerprint and profile reference are saved on-chain.

## What the delegate uses it for

The delegate reads each proposal against the brief and writes a vote and a reason. It uses the new brief from the next proposal after the fingerprint transaction lands.

## How you know it worked

The fingerprint changes when you save a new brief. The old fingerprint still proves which brief an earlier vote used. If the fingerprint transaction fails after private storage succeeds, retry that on-chain step without submitting the brief again.

## If nothing changes

Check that the brief saved, then open the pending vote. If the vote still shows the old reason, it was prepared before your change. Override it inside the window if you want a different result.
