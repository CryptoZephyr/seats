## You

The wallet that controls your seat can save a brief, change or cancel a pending vote before its deadline, open the vault while you hold the seat, and offer the seat for sale.

## Your delegate

Your delegate can prepare a vote and a one-line reason. Its key can request votes. It can't move your funds, override your choice, cancel a vote, or send the final vote before the change window closes.

## The vote service

A service sends the prepared vote after the window closes. It can't change the choice. A slow service can delay a cast.

## The club

The club decides who can join and whether to approve a sale. Club rules and contract changes are public.

## After a sale

The new holder controls the seat and writes a new brief. Your access to the seat and vault ends.
