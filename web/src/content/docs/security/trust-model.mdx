## The club

The club's DAO controls admission, sale approval, the model signer, and the levy. Members vote on each of these in the DAO.

## The member

The member controls the brief, can override or cancel a vote in the window, and can sell the seat through an approved sale.

## The delegate

The delegate prepares a vote and signs nothing on the member's behalf beyond its receipt. Its key can request votes and nothing else.

## NEAR AI

NEAR AI's inference service signs each answer. A club registers the signer, so the contract can check receipts against it.

## The keeper

The keeper requests votes and casts expired ones. It can't cast early, override, or move funds.

## The contract

The seat contract and the voter contract enforce the rules: the approved-sale gate, the window, the receipt check, and the payout. A change to any rule needs a new deployment.
