## You and the club

You control the brief and can change or cancel a pending vote during its 2-minute window. The club votes on membership applications and sale offers.

## Your delegate and NEAR AI

Your delegate reads the proposal and brief, then prepares a vote. NEAR AI creates and signs the response. The club's contract checks that signature. A signed response doesn't prove that the proposal was understood correctly.

## The public rules

The contracts enforce who controls a seat, when a vote can be changed, and how a sale is paid. A separate service can request a vote and send it after the window, but it can't override your choice.

## Hardware evidence
