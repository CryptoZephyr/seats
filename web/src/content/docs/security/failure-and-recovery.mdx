title: Failure and recovery
summary: What happens when a sale, a vote, or a cast doesn't go through.
lastVerified: 2026-11-06
commit: 886500f
---

## A sale that fails

If the controller change fails, the buyer is refunded in full. If the club declines or the sale times out, the buyer is refunded and the seller keeps the seat.

## A vote that is replayed

The voter contract rejects a receipt it has already seen.

## A cast that is late

A cast after the DAO's proposal period ends can be recorded as expired. The overridden result may still be recorded, as in the testnet run. Check the DAO record.

## A transaction hash you can't see

Some tool output doesn't print a hash for a failed call. The evidence pages mark those runs as not captured instead of guessing a hash.

## A stuck DAO

The testnet record documents one superseded attempt, where council votes weighed zero and the DAO couldn't act. That attempt is kept in the record, not hidden.
