## What a seat carries

A seat has a number and a holder. It carries one vote in the club's DAO, held by the seat's voter account, and it opens the vault while it's held.

## Vote weight

A seat's vote weight is 1 while it's held. The weight moves with the seat. When a seat is sold, the seller's voter account drops to 0, and the buyer's starts at 1.

## History

A seat keeps its history across sales. Minting, listings, sales, and votes stay attached to the seat number. Standing doesn't transfer. The buyer starts fresh on votes cast, even though the seat's past is visible.

## What ends when you sell

Your vote in the club's DAO, your vault access, your delegate and its brief, and your delegate's signing key all end when the sale completes. The testnet run confirmed the old key was removed.

## Limits

A seat can't be moved with a plain transfer or a marketplace listing. The seat contract refuses both.
