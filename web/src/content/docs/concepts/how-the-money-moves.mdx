## The steps

The buyer escrows the exact listed price. The club votes on the sale. If the club approves, the seat moves to the buyer, the seller receives 95% of the price, and the club's treasury receives the 5% levy. The buyer's vote weight starts at 1, and the seller's drops to 0.

If the club declines or the sale times out, the buyer is refunded in full and the seller keeps the seat.

## The split is shown before signing

The seller sees the 95/5 split before listing. The buyer sees the same split before escrowing.

## Atomicity

The ownership move, the voter-controller change, and the payouts are meant to succeed or fail together. If the controller change fails, the buyer is refunded in full. The testnet run recorded zero failed receipts.

## Example from testnet

A sale of token 1 for 0.5 NEAR paid the seller 0.475 NEAR and the DAO 0.025 NEAR. See [verified examples](/docs/evidence/verified-examples).
