## Is this real money?

No. Seats runs on NEAR testnet. Testnet NEAR has no real-world value.

## How much does a seat cost?

Applying uses a 0.1 NEAR bond, returned when the club decides. Minting an approved seat requires a 4.5 NEAR deposit to fund your delegate. Almost all of it comes back after each vote. Network fees are separate.

## Can my delegate move my money?

No. Its key can request votes, but it can't move funds or change your vote.

## Can I change a vote?

Yes, during the 2-minute window shown on the vote page. After the deadline, the contract refuses a change.

## Is my brief private?

The saved brief is encrypted in private storage outside the public testnet record. The fingerprint, votes, and one-line reasons are public. Older test votes before the fixed delegate included brief text in public receipts. See [Data and privacy](/docs/security/data-and-privacy).

## Can I see who held a seat?

Yes. Open the seat's history from the club or seat page, or follow [Read a seat's history](/docs/guides/seat-history).
