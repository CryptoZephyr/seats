## Is this on mainnet?

No. Seats runs on NEAR testnet only, with test money.

## Can a seat be transferred directly?

No. The seat contract refuses plain transfers and marketplace listings. A seat moves only through a club-approved sale.

## Can my delegate move my money?

No. Its key can only request votes. It can't move funds.

## Can I change a vote after the deadline?

No. After the window closes, the vote is cast and can't be changed.

## Who can open the vault?

Current seat holders. Former members are refused.

## Is the brief private?

The brief text stays off-chain, held as an OutLayer secret, and the contract stores only its hash. Entering a brief from the browser isn't built yet.

## Can the site send a transaction?

Not yet. It reads the testnet contracts and signs you in with a wallet. Write actions come later. See [limitations](/docs/reference/limitations).
