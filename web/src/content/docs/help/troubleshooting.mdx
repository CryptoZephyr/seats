## A page can't read testnet

Choose the page's retry action. If the error remains, try again later. Don't sign a second transaction until you check whether the first one landed.

## Your seat doesn't appear

Refresh the seat page. If you just minted, check the transaction link and wait for the public ownership record to update.

## The brief didn't update

Check the fingerprint. If the page offers `Save fingerprint on-chain (0.1 NEAR storage)`, use that rather than storing the brief again.

## The vote window is closed

The change period has ended. You can't override or cancel that vote. Open the proposal to read the recorded result.

## The vault refused your request

Only the current seat holder can open it. Sign in with the wallet that holds the seat.

## Your history won't load

Choose `Retry` on the history page. Holder and transaction records come from the testnet explorer and may be delayed.

## You need testnet NEAR

Use the [NEAR testnet faucet](https://near-faucet.io/). Testnet NEAR has no real-world value.
