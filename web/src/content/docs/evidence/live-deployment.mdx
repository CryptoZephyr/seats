## Testnet actions

The testnet app can send wallet requests for applications, seat minting, brief saves, vote changes, sales, purchases, and vault checks. Read the relevant guide before signing.

## Public evidence

The club and seat accounts are listed in the [testnet records](/docs/reference/deployment). Recorded transactions and examples are in [verified test runs](/docs/evidence/verified-examples).

## Limits

These records show that particular testnet actions ran. They don't prove that every future transaction or service request will succeed. Testnet NEAR has no real-world value.
