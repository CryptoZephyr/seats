## A vote and a change

Recorded testnet runs show a delegate voting Reject on an 80 NEAR proposal, and a member changing a pending Approve to Reject during the change window. The vote and short reason are public.

## A completed sale

A recorded 0.5 NEAR sale paid 0.475 NEAR to the seller and 0.025 NEAR to the club. The old holder lost vault access and the buyer became the seat holder.

## Vault check

A current holder received encrypted vault content. A former holder's request was refused.

## Limits

These are single testnet examples, not a promise about every future run. Older test votes made before the fixed delegate also exposed brief text in public receipts. See [Data and privacy](/docs/security/data-and-privacy).
