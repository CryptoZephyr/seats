## Network

Seats runs on NEAR testnet only. The club's tokens are for testing and have no real-world value.

## Public accounts

- Club voting account: `seats2-1793847972.sputnikv2.testnet`
- Seat contract: `seatsnft3-1793848334.testnet`
- Seat 1 vote account: `s1.seatsnft3-1793848334.testnet`
- Hardware-check account: `tdxv-1793838446.testnet`

These accounts are programs that enforce the club's rules. Paste an account or transaction ID into a NEAR testnet explorer to read its public record. The app also links to transactions after a successful action.

## What the records show

The public record can show who owns a seat, its brief fingerprint, votes, one-line reasons, and transactions. It can't prove that every future vote or service request will work.
