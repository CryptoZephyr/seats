## Vote states

Pending, window open: your delegate's vote is recorded and you can still override it. The screen shows the clock time and countdown.

Overridden, window open: you changed the vote inside the window. It will be cast at the deadline.

Window closed, waiting for cast: the deadline passed and the vote hasn't been cast yet. Anyone can trigger the cast.

Cast: the vote was sent to the DAO under the seat's voter account.

Approved, rejected, expired: the DAO's result for the proposal. Each status uses a word and a shape, never colour alone.

## Sale states

Listed for sale: the seat is on the club page with its price.

Offer waiting for club: a buyer escrowed the price, and the club hasn't voted.

Sale approved: the seat moved. The seller sees the payout, and the buyer sees the seat.

Declined or timed out: the buyer was refunded in full and the seller keeps the seat.

## Access states

Former member opening the vault: refused, with the message that only current seat holders can open the vault.

Late override refused: the window closed before the change arrived.

## Connection states

Network slow: the screen says what is waiting and how long it has been. It never shows a frozen spinner.

Wallet not connected: the screen offers a connect action and doesn't hide the state.
