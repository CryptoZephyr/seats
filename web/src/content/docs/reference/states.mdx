## Vote states

- `Pending`: your delegate has prepared a vote. The page shows a clock time and countdown while you can still change or cancel it.
- `Overridden`: you changed the prepared vote. It will be sent when the window closes.
- `Canceled`: your seat won't vote on this proposal.
- `Cast`: the vote was sent to the club's voting record.
- `Approved`, `Rejected`, or `Expired`: the proposal's result.

Each status uses a word and a shape. Don't rely on color alone. A vote change window lasts 2 minutes.

## Sale states

- `For sale`: the club approved an offer for the named buyer.
- `Awaiting club vote`: the club hasn't decided on the offer.
- Completed sale: the seat moved to the buyer, who paid the approved price.
- Declined or timed out: the buyer is refunded and the seller keeps the seat.

## Connection states

If the page can't read the testnet, it shows an error or loading state. Use the retry action and check the transaction record before trying a wallet request again.
