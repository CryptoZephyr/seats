## Testnet only

Seats has only run on NEAR testnet with test money. It hasn't been deployed to mainnet, and it hasn't been audited.

## The web app reads only

The app in `web/` reads testnet views and can submit Apply/Claim, Sell, Buy, and Vault transactions with a NEAR wallet. Brief editing and direct vote changes aren't part of these flows.

## Sign-in

Sign in with a NEAR testnet wallet. Email sign-in isn't built.

## Brief entry

In the testnet runs, the brief was held as an OutLayer secret, and the contract stored only its hash. Writing and saving a brief from the browser isn't built yet.

## Hardware attestation

The TDX quote and the GPU evidence are checked off-chain before a club registers the model signer. The contract checks the receipt against that signer, but it can't check the hardware quotes itself.

The hardware measurement covers the public OS. It doesn't show which inference containers served a given answer.

## The keeper

The keeper service runs on a free hosting tier, and anyone can cast a vote after its deadline. A slow keeper can delay a cast, but it can't cast early or change a vote.

## Demand

Real club demand hasn't been tested. The target is one real club and three operator quotes.
