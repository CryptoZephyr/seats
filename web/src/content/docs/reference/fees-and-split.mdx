## Sale split

The levy is 500 basis points, which is 5%. The seller receives the remaining 95%. The levy goes to the club's treasury. The split is fixed by the club and shown before you sign.

## Testnet measurements

A sale of 0.5 NEAR paid the seller 0.475 NEAR and the DAO 0.025 NEAR.

The voter contract used about 2.3 Tgas for a submit and about 8.2 Tgas for a cast, including the DAO call. The first cast attempt at 100 Tgas ran out of gas and succeeded at 300 Tgas.

Each voter account locked about 1.93 NEAR of storage in the voter spike. Testnet makes that free to use, but it would be a real cost on mainnet. Global contracts or a smaller voter contract could lower it.

## Key allowance

The delegate's signing key has a 1 NEAR allowance and can call only the vote request method.
