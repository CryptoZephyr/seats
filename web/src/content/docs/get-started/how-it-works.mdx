## Join the club

The design has the club pay for a new member's account, keys, gas, and the minted seat, so a newcomer doesn't need crypto first. On testnet, seats were minted through the club's DAO.

## Your delegate votes

You write a brief, a short set of rules like "back community events, reject any spend over 50 NEAR". Each open proposal goes to your delegate, which prepares a vote from the brief and gives a one-line reason. The vote comes with a signed receipt.

## You can override

The vote waits in a window before it counts. Inside the window you can change it. When the window closes, the vote is cast and can't be changed.

## Sell through the club

To sell, you set a price. A buyer escrows the exact price, and the club votes on the sale. If the club approves, the seat moves, you receive 95%, and the club receives 5%. If the club declines or the sale times out, the buyer is refunded in full.

## Open the vault

Current seat holders can open the club vault. The check runs inside secure hardware, and the content comes back encrypted to your own key.
