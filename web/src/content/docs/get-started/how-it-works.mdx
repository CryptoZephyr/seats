## Get a seat

Apply to join. The current testnet bond is 0.1 NEAR, returned when the club decides. After approval, choose `Mint your seat`. The 4.5 NEAR deposit funds your delegate. Almost all of it comes back after each vote.

## Your delegate prepares a vote

You write a brief with the rules you want followed. A proposal is a request the club votes on. For each proposal, your delegate prepares an Approve or Reject vote and a one-line reason. A proposal stays open for 10 minutes on testnet.

## Change or cancel a vote

When a prepared vote appears, a 2-minute window starts. The page shows the deadline as a clock time and a countdown. During that window, you can change the vote or cancel it. If you do nothing, the vote is sent when the window closes.

## Buy or sell a seat

The club must approve a sale before the named buyer can pay. When the sale completes, the seller receives 95% and the club receives 5%. The buyer starts with a fresh delegate brief.

## Open the vault and check history

Current seat holders can open the vault. A seat's public history shows who held it, its completed sales, and its recorded votes. See [seat history](/docs/guides/seat-history).
