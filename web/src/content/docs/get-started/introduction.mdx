## What Seats does

A club on Seats sells memberships as NFTs called seats. Each seat carries one vote in the club's Sputnik DAO and opens the club's private vault. A member can sell their seat, but only to a buyer the club approves.

## Who it is for

Members of a club, people who want to join, and the operators who set the club's rules. Each guide says who it is written for.

## What is live today

Seats runs on NEAR testnet only. The contracts, the testnet club, and the transactions in the evidence section are real, and you can check them on the explorer.

The web app in this repository is a visual design with sample data. It doesn't connect to a wallet or the chain yet, so the screens show how a step looks, not that it ran.

## What this site doesn't prove

A screen doesn't prove that a vote was cast or that money moved. The transaction links and the evidence section show what actually ran.
