## What you can do

Seats gives you a membership seat in a club. You can apply, mint your seat after approval, write rules for your delegate, review its votes, sell your seat through club approval, open the vault, and read a seat's history.

[Open the Seats testnet app](https://seats-near.vercel.app).

## Before you start

Seats runs on NEAR testnet. The tokens are for testing and have no real-world value. Use a NEAR testnet wallet. You may need testnet NEAR for the application bond, seat deposit, and network fees. [Get testnet NEAR from the faucet](https://near-faucet.io/).

## What others can see

Seat owners, brief fingerprints, votes, one-line reasons, and transaction records are public. Your saved brief is encrypted in private storage, but older test votes exposed brief text in public vote receipts. Read [Data and privacy](/docs/security/data-and-privacy) before writing a brief.

These pages describe the testnet app. They don't promise that every request will succeed. If a wallet or service is slow, check the transaction record before trying again.
